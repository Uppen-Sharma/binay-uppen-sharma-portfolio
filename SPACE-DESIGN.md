# Space, with a clear way in — 13 October 2026

The user's brief: the previous visual direction was appealing, but visitors could not tell what was clickable. Use space/science-fiction interests without obscuring the portfolio.

## Direction

Midnight blue (#090e1c), slate surfaces, ice-blue actions (#b9e4f1), and a small warm orbital accent. Original SVG planet and orbital rings; no copied reference assets, stock portrait, heavy WebGL scene, autoplay or scroll hijacking. Syne display type and self-hosted Manrope body type remain. Full name, JavaScript/MERN focus, verified project data and sanitized company contributions remain unchanged.

Atmospheric inspiration: [Awwwards Sites of the Year](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/), especially [Star Atlas](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/star-atlas). These are references for atmosphere and composition, not templates or borrowed assets. The frontend-design skill guided intentional visual hierarchy; the established animation-performance approach keeps motion on transforms and opacity.

## Affordances

- Hero states the developer's full name and work, then offers **View projects** and **GitHub profile**.
- Every featured project has a visible **View case study** button. Thumbnail artwork is decorative and does not react to pointer input.
- Archive rows include a bordered **View details** label, plus hover and keyboard-focus feedback.
- The analytics preview is a real, labelled Context/SQL/Answer control with a selected state and 44px targets. It is identified as illustrative, not customer data.
- Projects, About and Contact stay visible on mobile. The closing heading is not a disguised link; explicit contact and GitHub actions sit below it.
- Native dialogs retain Escape, close-button handling, modal background isolation and focus return. Reduced-motion preferences remain supported.

No invented achievements, metrics or credentials. No new personal data exposure.
